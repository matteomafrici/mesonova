## Checklist

- [ ] **Requirements analysis** — the requirement set, with its sources, its rationale and how each
      one will be verified
- [ ] **Functional analysis** — what the vehicle does, phase by phase, and the functions everything
      else is traced against
- [ ] **Baseline selection** — a configuration chosen and declared, with the criteria and the numbers
      behind the choice
