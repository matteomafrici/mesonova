# Launch Systems 2026–27 — Project Assignment, Option 1

> **Source of truth.** The text below is the assignment as provided, reproduced verbatim. Do not
> edit it. Project documents cite its statements as items **A1–A20**, in order of appearance
> (see `work/pm1/requirements/requirements.md` §1).

---

Design an air-launched sounding rocket for in-situ measurements in the mesosphere. The vehicle
could have a single or multi-stage configuration. The vehicle shall carry a single- or
multi-instrument scientific payload for measurements of atmospheric composition, metallic ions,
aerosols, or particulate matter.

The following requirements shall be taken into account:

- Maximum payload mass: mpay = 250 kg
- Required apogee: 110 ≤ hapo ≤ 125 km
- Maximum rocket mass at aircraft release: GLOM = 1750 kg

Scientific payload mass and volume shall be derived from literature and existing
payload/instrument baselines. The allowable payload acceleration and thermal environment shall be
derived from the selected payload. The vehicle shall provide at least a 20% margin with respect
to the maximum allowable payload acceleration. The payload shall acquire useful scientific data
while crossing the 70-100 km altitude range. The total useful measurement time in this region
shall be quantified.

The carrier aircraft shall be selected by the design team and must be fully compatible with the
sounding rocket. The effects of release conditions and other uncertain parameters on performance
and trajectory shall be investigated.

The scientific payload shall be reusable. Ground impact of payload shall satisfy a casualty risk
lower than 10-4.

Consider expendable launch vehicle stages.

Carrier aircraft, launch/recovery area, technologies, and operating countries shall be compatible
with realistic technology-access and geopolitical constraints.

The design shall rely primarily on high-TRL technologies, requiring only minor developments (AD2
to be quantified). No budget constraints are foreseen (i.e., project cost is not a driver).

The project shall include preliminary configuration selection, baseline identification,
requirements analysis, functional analysis, mass sizing, staging, aerodynamics, stability,
trajectory, structural assessment, recovery and uncertainty analyses.
